# Messenger of the Frost

Welcome to **Messenger of the Frost**, a narrative-driven exploration game built with GameMaker. Step into the shoes of Mari, a girl who wakes up in a frozen world with a mysterious destiny.

## The Story

The world of **Icoris** is dying. An eternal freeze is slowly consuming everything, blanketing the land in thick, unforgiving snow. Hope is fading from the hearts of its last inhabitants.

You play as **Mari**, who wakes up in a remote cabin with no memory but a glowing book by her side. After a chance encounter with a scavenger named **Ko** and a conversation with a literal **Angel Statue**, you learn a startling truth: You are the Messiah. Your mission? To rekindle hope in the people of Icoris and show them that their world can still be saved.

## Meet the Characters

*   **Mari**: The reluctant messiah. Driven by curiosity and a growing sense of responsibility, she explores Icoris to gather "Hope."
*   **Ko**: A cheerful scavenger trying to make the best of her time in a dying world. She's your first friend and provides the key to exploring further.
*   **Trent**: An engineer struggling to keep his robots functional amidst the cold. He represents the technical ingenuity still left in Icoris.
*   **Seira**: A passionate artist living in humanity's last bastion. She seeks beauty even as the world around her freezes.
*   **The Angel Statue**: A mysterious, glowing entity that gives Mari her mission and the tools to succeed.

## Key Mechanics

*   **The Hope Jar**: Your primary objective. By helping the people of Icoris and proving that the snow can be melted with **Yellow Dust**, you fill the jar with the collective hope of the world.
*   **Exploration & Puzzle Solving**: Traverse through snowy wastes, warehouses, caves, and city apartments to find essential items and progress the story.
*   **Narrative Choice**: Your interactions with the inhabitants of Icoris shape the journey towards multiple possible endings.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **W, A, S, D** | Move Mari around the world |
| **Shift** | Run |
| **Space** | Interact with objects / Advance dialogue |
| **Tab** | Open Inventory |
| **M** | Check the **Hope Jar** progress |
| **Esc** | Exit the game |

## Getting Started

### Play the Game
Simply run the `Messenger of the Frost.exe` file located in the root directory to start your journey.

### Development / Modding
To view or modify the source code:
1.  Install [GameMaker](https://gamemaker.io/).
2.  Open the `Messenger of the Frost.yyp` project file.
3.  Explore the `objects`, `scripts`, and `rooms` folders to understand the game logic.
